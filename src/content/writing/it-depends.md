Since Twitter died, I've been fond of the habit of canonising its truly immortal contributions to public thought, or at least to our most persistent stims. An eternal favourite of mine will be:

![An immortal tweet: "people b saying things so definitively. like man i think it depends"](assets/it-depends)

I've realised it's basically the foundation of a little nexus of thoughts I've wanted to write about. One can find a more erudite write-up in the short essay [Reality has a surprising amount of detail](https://johnsalvatier.org/blog/2017/reality-has-a-surprising-amount-of-detail) by John Salvatier. It also connects to a loose maxim that I've been polishing the more grand unified theories of software delivery management I encounter, which goes something like "General solutions are better than most specific solutions, but never better than the right specific solution for you".
